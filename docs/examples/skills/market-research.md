Research a market event before judging it.

1. Identify what actually happened and when, from at least two independent sources.
2. Compare it with the holding's entry in your state (thesis, baseline, open concerns).
3. Classify it: noise, worth recording, worth the user's attention, or needs the user's action.
4. Update your state with anything you will need next time. Use a dedup_key of `<symbol>:<what>:<date>` when you notify.
